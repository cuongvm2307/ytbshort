YTBShort

A private, self-hosted tool that schedules YouTube Shorts on the operator's own channels.

What it does

YTBShort runs on a single Windows computer owned by its operator. It helps the operator publish short gaming videos on two YouTube channels that the operator owns:

  • Edits the operator's own gameplay recordings (and Creative Commons or explicitly licensed clips, with credit) into vertical 9:16 Shorts with captions and on-screen text.
  • Uploads each finished Short to the operator's own channel as private, with a scheduled publish time (videos.insert with status.publishAt).
  • Checks the upload status of its own videos (videos.list) to confirm they were published.

Who can use it

YTBShort is not a public service. There is no sign-up and no other users: only the operator signs in, with the Google accounts that own their two channels. It does not access, collect or analyze other people's YouTube accounts or data.
